About Our School

Air's University of Mandaluyong is a premier academic institution committed to nurturing learners and preparing them for the challenges of modern society. With a strong tradition of discipline, innovation, and excellence, the university continues to build a community where students thrive academically, socially, and emotionally.

Our campus offers a wide array of opportunities, including highly skilled educators, advanced facilities, and programs designed to shape students into competent and responsible leaders of tomorrow.

Today, Air’s University stands as one of the most promising educational institutions in Mandaluyong — a place where dreams take flight.

Why Is It Called Air's University?

The name "Air's University"comes from the nickname of the school’s founder, Aaron James Dee. When you pronounce the name Aaron, it naturally sounds like "Air". To honor the founder’s dedication, leadership, and vision, the school proudly carries the name Air's University of Mandaluyong. The name symbolizes freedom, growth, and limitless potential — reminding every student that like air, their future has no limits.
